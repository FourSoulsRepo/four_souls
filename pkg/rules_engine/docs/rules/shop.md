# Purchasing and slots

Sources: S-OFF Purchasing, Refilling Slots; S-RU Покупка, Заполнение слотов.

1. **R-SHOP-01** The active player may declare a purchase in their action phase while the stack is empty (so not during an attack or while their death is pending).
2. **R-SHOP-02** Declaring a purchase passes priority before the item is chosen: a shop item or the top card of the treasure deck.
3. **R-SHOP-03** The price is then fixed; by default 10¢ for either choice.
4. **R-SHOP-04** If the player cannot pay, the purchase fails; otherwise they pay and gain the item.
5. **R-SHOP-05** By default one purchase per turn.
6. **R-SHOP-06** Every shop, monster and room slot must hold at least one card; an empty slot is refilled from its deck the next time a player would get priority.
7. **R-SHOP-07** A monster slot emptied by a death refills after all death steps and the triggers from that death have resolved.
8. **R-SHOP-08** Events revealed while refilling are resolved one after another until a monster sits in the slot.
9. **R-SHOP-09** The active player refills slots and resolves those events even while dead.
