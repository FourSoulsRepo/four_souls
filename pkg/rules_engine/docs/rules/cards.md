# Cards

Sources: S-OFF Card Types; S-RU Типы карт.

## Treasure

1. **R-CARD-01** A treasure card in play is an **item**. Items are controlled by a player or sit in the shop.
2. **R-CARD-02** To gain treasure, take that many cards from the top of the treasure deck into play under your control.
3. **R-CARD-03** A starting item is an item and has the Eternal keyword.
4. **R-CARD-04** Gold border = active item (has activated abilities); silver border = passive item. The border itself has no rules meaning.
5. **R-CARD-05** Shop items' abilities do not work in the shop, except triggers on entering or leaving play.

## Loot

6. **R-CARD-06** To loot N, draw N cards from the top of the loot deck into your hand.
7. **R-CARD-07** A loot card in hand is played onto the stack, where it is a **loot**; when it resolves its loot ability happens, then it goes to the loot discard.
8. **R-CARD-08** Playing a loot card needs priority and a loot play, unless an ability says otherwise.

## Monsters and events

9. **R-CARD-09** A monster card in play with a stat block is a **monster**; without one it is an **event**. Both sit in monster slots.
10. **R-CARD-10** "You" on a monster card means the active player.
11. **R-CARD-11** Monster card abilities work only in play, and not while the card is a soul, unless stated.
12. **R-CARD-12** Monsters can be attacked unless stated otherwise; events cannot be attacked.
13. **R-CARD-13** When a monster dies, the active player gets its rewards, whoever killed it.
14. **R-CARD-14** A boss is a monster with a soul icon; when it dies the active player gains it as a soul.
15. **R-CARD-15** An event's abilities are triggered abilities that trigger when it enters play; after they resolve or are cancelled, it goes to the monster discard.
16. **R-CARD-16** If an event leaves play, its abilities are removed from the stack.
17. **R-CARD-17** A curse is an event with the Curse keyword (R-ABIL-20).

## Souls

18. **R-CARD-18** A card held as a **soul** gives its controller its soul value (the icon, or a value set by an effect).
19. **R-CARD-19** The number of souls a player controls is public.
20. **R-CARD-20** A soul is one object whatever its value; a soul worth 2 is destroyed or stolen as one.
21. **R-CARD-21** Abilities that count souls count total soul value; gaining a soul worth N counts as gaining N souls for triggers.

## Bonus souls

22. **R-CARD-22** Active bonus souls are not in play before they are gained.
23. **R-CARD-23** A bonus soul is gained as soon as its condition is met, by the player who meets it; it then works like any soul and its own abilities stop.
24. **R-CARD-24** Each bonus soul can be gained once per game; a destroyed bonus soul is set aside face down for good.

## Characters

25. **R-CARD-25** A character card in play is a **character**; its stat box (HP, ATK) is its controller's stats.
26. **R-CARD-26** A character's starting item enters play under its controller when the character enters play, without using the stack.
27. **R-CARD-27** A character with a soul value is not a soul.

## Card anatomy

28. **R-CARD-28** A stat box has up to three stats: health (HP), evasion (dice check, DC) and attack (ATK).
29. **R-CARD-29** Gray text is a reminder or flavor and has no rules effect.
30. **R-CARD-30** The tags ↷ and $ mark activated abilities; level tags mark which abilities an object has at a level.
31. **R-CARD-31** When an object with a reward box dies or is destroyed, the active player gets the rewards, unless stated.
32. **R-CARD-32** Cards with the 3+ player symbol are removed in 1- and 2-player games; one found during play is set aside and replaced by another card of the same type (GS-08).
33. **R-CARD-33** *(later sets)* Rooms sit in room slots; only the active player uses a room's activated abilities.
