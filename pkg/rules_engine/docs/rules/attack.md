# Attacking

Sources: S-OFF Attacking; S-RU Атака.

1. **R-ATK-01** The active player may declare an attack in their action phase while the stack is empty.
2. **R-ATK-02** Declaring an attack passes priority before a target is chosen; then the player picks a monster in a slot or the top of the monster deck (or another attackable object, R-MECH-04).
3. **R-ATK-03** An effect that forces an attack on a target must be obeyed while it applies; if two force different targets, the attacker picks one.
4. **R-ATK-04** Abilities may react to the declaration itself (before the target) or to a declaration against a target (after it).
5. **R-ATK-05** Abilities that look for an attack on a monster do not react to an attack on the monster deck, even if a monster is revealed.
6. **R-ATK-06** "When an attack is declared on this" works both for a card already in a slot and for a card revealed from the monster deck.
7. **R-ATK-07** Killing a monster outside an attack is not an attack.
8. **R-ATK-08** Attacking the monster deck: reveal the top card and put it on a monster slot of your choice, covering what is there.
9. **R-ATK-09** If the revealed card is an event, its abilities trigger and it then goes to discard; the attack is over.
10. **R-ATK-10** If the revealed card cannot be attacked, it stays in the slot and the attack is cancelled.
11. **R-ATK-11** An attack is a series of attack rolls (D6) against the target until it ends; it cannot be stopped voluntarily.
12. **R-ATK-12** An attack roll equal to or above the target's evasion hits: the attacker's ATK in combat damage goes on the stack aimed at the target.
13. **R-ATK-13** A lower roll misses: the target's ATK in combat damage goes on the stack aimed at the attacker.
14. **R-ATK-14** The attack ends when the attacker or target dies, when either has 0 HP before the next roll, or when it is cancelled (also by the turn ending).
15. **R-ATK-15** When an attack ends, its unresolved attack rolls and combat damage are removed from the stack.
16. **R-ATK-16** A player told to roll an attack roll who is not the attacker does not end the attack by dying; dead players make no attack rolls.
17. **R-ATK-17** If a player and a monster take lethal damage at the same moment, the monster's death resolves first.
18. **R-ATK-18** Evasion is between 1 and 6.
