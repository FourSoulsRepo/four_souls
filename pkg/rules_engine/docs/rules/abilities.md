# Abilities and effects

Sources: S-OFF Abilities, Effects; S-RU Способности, Эффекты.

## General

1. **R-ABIL-01** Abilities are the effect text on a card; gray text is not an ability.
2. **R-ABIL-02** Abilities work only while their object is in play, unless stated.
3. **R-ABIL-03** An ability already on the stack still resolves if its object leaves play (loot abilities are different: they happen when the loot resolves).
4. **R-ABIL-04** Targets and "choose one-" options are picked when the ability goes on the stack, not when it resolves.
5. **R-ABIL-05** Targets are the parts written after ↷ or $, after a trigger, or in a loot's first sentence up to the first period; everything else is decided on resolution.
6. **R-ABIL-06** If a target is gone or no longer valid on resolution, the ability fizzles.
7. **R-ABIL-07** Text before a colon is a cost, except in roll abilities ("1-2: …"). The cost must be paid for the rest to happen.

## Ability types

8. **R-ABIL-08** Activated abilities can be used whenever the player has priority and can pay the cost, unless stated.
9. **R-ABIL-09** Only an item's or character's controller uses its activated abilities.
10. **R-ABIL-10** A ↷ ability costs deactivating its object, so the object must be charged.
11. **R-ABIL-11** A $ ability has a cost other than ↷ and can be used while deactivated, if the cost can be paid.
12. **R-ABIL-12** Static abilities are always true and never use the stack.
13. **R-ABIL-13** Triggered abilities start with "when", "whenever", "at", or timing words like "each time", "the first time".
14. **R-ABIL-14** A triggered ability goes on the stack the next time any player would get priority.
15. **R-ABIL-15** Several triggers at once: the game's triggers first, in an order the active player picks; then each player's triggers in turn order from the active player; each player orders their own.
16. **R-ABIL-16** A trigger with a circled number fires on any roll that resolves as that number, whatever the roll was for.
17. **R-ABIL-17** Any ability on a loot card that is not triggered, activated or static is a loot ability, done when the loot resolves.

## Keywords

18. **R-ABIL-18** **Eternal**: the object cannot be destroyed or put into discard. Works only in play.
19. **R-ABIL-19** **Trinket** (loot): when the loot resolves it becomes an item you gain. Its other text works only in play.
20. **R-ABIL-20** **Curse**: when it enters play the active player picks a player who gains it; when that player dies, the curse goes to discard.
21. **R-ABIL-21** **Ambush** (loot): when the loot resolves, it becomes a monster in a chosen monster slot, and the active player must attack it once more this turn.
22. **R-ABIL-22** **Guppy**: no effect; a marker other cards look for.
23. **R-ABIL-23** **Indomitable** (monster): instead of going into a monster slot it expands monster slots by 1 and takes the new slot; it cannot be covered.
24. **R-ABIL-24** **Roll-**: makes a dice roll plus a trigger that reads its result; both go on the stack after the ability resolves, the trigger after the roll is made. The roller controls both.
25. **R-ABIL-25** *(later sets)* **Team Up**: after each attack roll of the active player against it, each other chosen player makes an attack roll in turn order, until the attack ends.
26. **R-ABIL-26** *(later sets)* **Familiar**: this item can be attacked.

## Effects

27. **R-ABIL-27** A continuous effect from a triggered or activated ability lasts until the end point it names, or forever if none.
28. **R-ABIL-28** A continuous effect from a static ability lasts while its object is in the zone where it works.
29. **R-ABIL-29** Replacement effects change an event before it happens and never use the stack.
30. **R-ABIL-30** They are marked by "instead", "prevent", "skip", "this enters play …" and "as …". "Prevent" and "skip" replace the event with nothing.
31. **R-ABIL-31** "This enters play …" and "as …" add to the event rather than replacing it (e.g. "enters play deactivated").
32. **R-ABIL-32** A replacement effect applies at most once to the same event.
33. **R-ABIL-33** If several replacement effects apply to one event, the affected player picks their order; one may stop another from applying.
