# Keyword actions

Sources: S-OFF Specific Mechanics; S-RU Специальные механики.

1. **R-MECH-01** **Activate**: put a ↷ or $ ability on the stack by deactivating its object or paying its cost.
2. **R-MECH-02** **And / then**: "A and B" does B only if A happened; "A, then B" and "A. B." do B either way.
3. **R-MECH-03** **If you do**: the next part happens only if the previous part took effect.
4. **R-MECH-04** Attackable items and rooms have stat blocks and can be chosen when declaring an attack; they are not monsters.
5. **R-MECH-05** An attackable object killed in an attack is destroyed; the active player gets its rewards even if it is destroyed outside an attack.
6. **R-MECH-06** When an ability lets players attack players, it sets the target's evasion; if several do, the most recent one wins.
7. **R-MECH-07** **Cancel**: remove something from the stack without effect. A cancelled death heals the object to at least 1 HP, unless stated.
8. **R-MECH-08** **Copy an object**: it takes the copied card's abilities, name, stat block, reward box and soul icon, and loses its own. Counters, charge state, damage, back face and starting-item status are not copied.
9. **R-MECH-09** **Copy a loot** (on the stack): a new loot appears on the stack with new targets and choices; costs are not paid again. A copy only exists on the stack.
10. **R-MECH-10** **Copy a loot card**: the copy waits in a holding zone until played or until its window ends; playing it needs a loot play and its costs.
11. **R-MECH-11** **Copy an ability**: the ability goes on the stack without costs; a loot copying a "destroy this" ability fizzles.
12. **R-MECH-12** **Counters** do nothing by themselves; named counters are counters too. They stay with the object when it changes control and are removed when it leaves play (being covered counts).
13. **R-MECH-13** Counters on a player stay when their character changes; counters on a character card do not.
14. **R-MECH-14** {HP}, {ATK} and Eternal counters give +1 HP, +1 ATK and Eternal while they are on the object.
15. **R-MECH-15** **Damage** is put on the stack aimed at an object with HP; when it resolves, that much damage is marked.
16. **R-MECH-16** HP never goes below 0; an object at 0 HP takes no more marked damage.
17. **R-MECH-17** The source of damage counts as having dealt it only when it resolves.
18. **R-MECH-18** "Take N damage" without a target means you; the source is the game.
19. **R-MECH-19** Damage marked on a player stays with the player if their character changes or flips.
20. **R-MECH-20** Nobody deals or takes 0 damage: nothing goes on the stack.
21. **R-MECH-21** Lowering max HP also removes that much marked damage; this is not healing.
22. **R-MECH-22** **Deactivate**: turn an object sideways. **Recharge**: turn it upright.
23. **R-MECH-23** **Destroy / kill**: set HP to 0 if it has HP; otherwise, or if it does not die, move it to discard. Characters and Eternal objects never go to discard.
24. **R-MECH-24** **Die**: the game destroys or kills an object; a destroyed or killed object dies.
25. **R-MECH-25** **Discard** (action): move loot cards of your choice from hand to the loot discard.
26. **R-MECH-26** **Double ¢**: gain as many ¢ as you have.
27. **R-MECH-27** **Each player**: players act in turn order from the ability's controller (or the active player for the game's abilities).
28. **R-MECH-28** "Each player dies / takes damage" is put on the stack in reverse turn order, so it resolves in turn order.
29. **R-MECH-29** For "each monster" or objects one player controls, that controller (or the active player) picks the order; shop items come last.
30. **R-MECH-30** "Each player and each monster dies": monsters first, then players.
31. **R-MECH-31** **Expand slots**: add slots of that type; new slots behave like the others and must be refilled.
32. **R-MECH-32** **Fizzle**: the game cancels something whose targets became invalid; cancelling an attack fizzles its unresolved rolls and combat damage.
33. **R-MECH-33** **Flip an object**: turn it to its back face, if it has one; it stays the same object and only the visible face counts.
34. **R-MECH-34** **Flip a dice roll**: the result becomes 7 minus the result.
35. **R-MECH-35** **Gain** and **give** differ: stealing and swapping count as giving, never as gaining.
36. **R-MECH-36** Gain ¢: from the game's pool to yours. Gain an object: move it under your control.
37. **R-MECH-37** Give: you move an object, loot card or ¢ of your choice to another player (or the shop, for items).
38. **R-MECH-38** Steal: the other player gives you an object, loot card or ¢ of your choice.
39. **R-MECH-39** Swap: both players give each other an object, or a loot card, at the same time.
40. **R-MECH-40** **Heal**: remove damage (a set amount, or all). Never above max HP; not while the object's death is on the stack unless that death is prevented or cancelled.
41. **R-MECH-41** **Levels**: level up adds a level counter, level down removes one; level tags say which abilities apply, and lower tiers keep working at higher levels.
42. **R-MECH-42** **Lose ¢**: move ¢ to the game's pool; if you have fewer, lose all you have.
43. **R-MECH-43** **Note**: remember a value for an ability; it belongs to the object that noted it and is cleared when that object goes to discard.
44. **R-MECH-44** **Pay**: you can only pay what you have. Pay ¢ moves ¢ (to the game's pool unless stated); pay HP loses that much HP.
45. **R-MECH-45** **Play**: move a loot card to the stack; you control it.
46. **R-MECH-46** **Prevent damage** removes that damage from the stack; **prevent death** removes the death and heals the object to at least 1 HP, unless stated.
47. **R-MECH-47** **Reroll a dice roll**: roll it again; it changes the same roll, not a new one.
48. **R-MECH-48** **Reroll an item**: destroy it; if you do, its controller gains 1 treasure. A shop item is replaced by the top treasure card in its slot instead.
49. **R-MECH-49** **Vote**: the named players vote in turn order from the ability's controller (or the active player); a vote cannot be changed.
50. **R-MECH-50** *(later sets)* **Additional names**: "This is also named …" works in every zone.
