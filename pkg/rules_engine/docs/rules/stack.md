# Priority, the stack and dice

Sources: S-OFF Priority, The Stack, Dice Rolls; S-RU Приоритет, Стек, Броски кубика.

## Priority

1. **R-PRIO-01** Priority decides who may act. Only a player with priority plays loot or uses activated abilities.
2. **R-PRIO-02** When priority passes, the active player gets it first, then each other player in turn order.
3. **R-PRIO-03** When a player puts a loot, ability or roll on the stack, priority passes starting with that player, then in turn order.
4. **R-PRIO-04** A player with priority may take any number of actions before passing.
5. **R-PRIO-05** The game moves on only when every player passes in a row.
6. **R-PRIO-06** Nobody has priority before the recharge step or after end-phase step 1.

## The stack

7. **R-STACK-01** Loot, abilities, dice rolls, damage and deaths go on the stack before they take effect.
8. **R-STACK-02** The stack resolves last in, first out.
9. **R-STACK-03** The top item resolves when all players pass priority in a row, starting from the player who put it there, and nobody adds anything.
10. **R-STACK-04** Priority passes after each item resolves, so players can respond between resolutions.
11. **R-STACK-05** An item cannot be interrupted while it resolves.
12. **R-STACK-06** A removed or cancelled item does not resolve; a removed loot goes where the removing ability says, a cancelled loot to the loot discard.
13. **R-STACK-07** When all players pass on an empty stack, the game moves to the next step or phase (not out of the action phase, R-TURN-09).
14. **R-STACK-08** Triggered abilities wait and go on the stack the next time a player would get priority (R-ABIL-14).

## Dice rolls

15. **R-DICE-01** Rolls use a D6 unless stated; a result never goes above 6 or below 1.
16. **R-DICE-02** A roll is rolled first, then put on the stack; several dice are rolled together.
17. **R-DICE-03** Then "when a dice is rolled" triggers trigger.
18. **R-DICE-04** While it is on the stack, abilities and loot may change the roll.
19. **R-DICE-05** When all players pass, the roll tries to resolve: "would roll N" triggers trigger now; if one changes the result, go back to R-DICE-04.
20. **R-DICE-06** As the roll resolves, continuous effects that modify rolls apply; the result is then final, and "when you roll N" triggers trigger.
21. **R-DICE-07** A "would roll N" trigger fizzles if, when it resolves, the roll is gone or no longer N (S-YUG).
22. **R-DICE-08** *(Gold Box)* **Twice in a row**: two final results of one player's rolls this turn are equal, with no roll of that player between them.
    1. Each such pair counts once: three equal rolls in a row are two pairs (S-DIS).
    2. When one of several dice is chosen as the result, only the chosen result is a roll (S-DIS).
    3. "Of one player" is our proposal: Q-10.
23. **R-DICE-09** *(Four Souls+)* **Twice in a row on an attack roll**: two of one player's attack rolls this turn, one right after the other, have equal final results.
    1. Only attack rolls count; that player's other rolls between them do not break the pair (Q-12).
    2. Compare R-DICE-08: there every roll of the player counts.
    3. Example: attack roll 3, Fruitcake rolls 5, attack roll 3: Euthanasia kills; Fruitcake does not recharge.
24. **R-DICE-10** *(Four Souls+)* An ability that lets a player change a roll by an amount ("add or subtract 1", PHD) changes each roll at most once (Q-11).
25. **R-DICE-11** *(Four Souls+)* **Several dice, one result** (Mutant Spider): the dice are rolled at once; only the chosen die is a roll, as R-DICE-08 says (S-DIS, Reddit).
26. **R-DICE-12** *(Requiem)* A replacement effect on a roll ("if a player would roll a 2 or 3, it becomes a 1 instead": Eternal D6) applies as the roll resolves, after "would roll" triggers; it can undo their change (S-YUG).
