# Co-op and challenges

Sources: S-MAE (Solitaire and Co-op Rules, Challenge Rules, How the Krampus Stole Christmas); S-OFF Solitaire/Co-op Mode, Four Souls Challenges; S-YUG.

Owner decisions (2026-11-10): a "Challenge" game mode beside the regular game; co-op only, exactly 2 people with one character each.
Solo play (one person, two characters) comes later (roadmap 14.27); so does the competitive Krampus (14.28).

## Co-op

1. **R-COOP-01** A co-op game has exactly 2 players, each with one character. For abilities and targeting each character is a separate player.
2. **R-COOP-02** Setup is the regular setup (R-SETUP-01 to R-SETUP-12, R-SETUP-16) without bonus souls. At the end a D8, the **timer**, is put out showing 8.
3. **R-COOP-03** Cards with the 3+ player symbol are out of the game (R-CARD-32).
4. **R-COOP-04** The first player is chosen as usual (R-SETUP-11); the turn order then stays the same all game.
5. **R-COOP-05** Each player keeps their own ¢, hand and souls; nothing is pooled. There is no limit to the souls one player may hold.
6. **R-COOP-06** Players may not trade ¢ with each other.
7. **R-COOP-07** The official rules let players keep their hands face up; we show each player the partner's hand (Q-42).
8. **R-COOP-08** The timer ticks down by 1 at the end of each round, when both players have had their turn. The round is the game's round, counted from the starting player (R-TURN-15, R-TURN-16, Q-34).
9. **R-COOP-09** When the timer reaches 0, both players lose at once.
10. **R-COOP-10** Both players win together or lose together; nobody wins alone.
11. **R-COOP-11** With rooms: after the room step of the end phase (R-TURN-12), a room whose top card has stayed in its slot for a whole round goes to discard, and the slot refills (Q-38, Q-39).
12. **R-COOP-12** Plain co-op (goal: 4 souls between both players; the timer also drops when a player dies on their own turn, Q-04) is not offered. Co-op is played only as a challenge, which sets the goal (R-CHAL-14).

## Challenges

13. **R-CHAL-01** A challenge is a co-op game (COOP) with its own setup, goal and global modifiers, at a difficulty picked at setup. Its rules add to the general ones; where they disagree, the challenge wins.
14. **R-CHAL-02** **Final boss slot**: a slot row apart from the monster slots. A challenge's setup puts its final boss there.
15. **R-CHAL-03** A monster in a final boss slot is a monster and a **final boss**. Abilities that choose monsters may choose it, and the active player may declare an attack on it.
16. **R-CHAL-04** A final boss can't be covered and can't leave its slot unless it is killed, unless the challenge says otherwise (Q-37). Effects that would move it do nothing to it.
17. **R-CHAL-05** **Boss armor**: if a final boss would be killed outright by an ability (not by damage), or would take more than 3 non-combat damage in one instance, it takes 3 damage instead. Combat damage is never capped.
18. **R-CHAL-06** A final boss that is killed dies as usual (R-DEATH-04 to R-DEATH-09); the challenge says what follows.
19. **R-CHAL-07** **Minion slots**: a slot row apart from the monster slots. A monster in a minion slot is a monster and a **minion**; abilities that choose monsters may choose it, and the active player may declare an attack on it.
20. **R-CHAL-08** "Minion" and "minions" mean monsters in play in minion slots, and nothing else (S-YUG).
21. **R-CHAL-09** A card goes into a minion slot only when an ability says so. Each such card gets a new minion slot of its own.
22. **R-CHAL-10** A minion can't leave its slot unless it is killed, unless the challenge says otherwise.
23. **R-CHAL-11** A minion dies as usual. Minion slots are never refilled: a slot is removed when its card leaves.
24. **R-CHAL-12** An event put in a minion slot does its abilities, then goes to discard as in a monster slot; its slot is then removed, so the challenge has one minion less (S-YUG).
25. **R-CHAL-13** **Timer**: in a challenge it ticks down only at the end of each round (R-COOP-08) or when an ability says so. It never ticks down on a death (Q-35).
26. **R-CHAL-14** **Beating a challenge**: the challenge is won only when its goal is met. Effects that would make a player win or end the game do nothing; the soul target (R-WIN-01) does not apply.
27. **R-CHAL-15** The challenge is lost when the timer reaches 0 or when its own loss condition is met. The game ends at once on a win or a loss.

## How the Krampus Stole Christmas

28. **R-KRAMPUS-01** **Goal**: the challenge is won when Pilfering Krampus dies. It is lost when all 4 presents are destroyed, or when the timer reaches 0.
29. **R-KRAMPUS-02** Difficulties: Normal, Hard and Ultra Hard. Each has its own Pilfering Krampus card and its own rules below.
30. **R-KRAMPUS-03** Pilfering Krampus by difficulty (the boss cards):

| Difficulty | HP | Evasion | ATK | Reward | Soul |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | 5 | 4+ | 2 | +1 treasure | 1 |
| Hard | 6 | 4+ | 2 | +1 treasure | 1 |
| Ultra Hard | 6 | 4+ | 2 | +1 treasure | 1 |

31. **R-KRAMPUS-04** **Setup**: the co-op setup (R-COOP-02), then Pilfering Krampus of the chosen difficulty goes face up into a final boss slot.
32. **R-KRAMPUS-05** Then the top 4 treasure cards go face down beside the play area as the 4 **presents**: the ¢ present, the loot present, the treasure present and the soul present. Each starts with counters by difficulty:

| Difficulty | ¢ | Loot | Treasure | Soul |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | 8 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| Hard | 12 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| Ultra Hard | 16 | 4 | 3 | 3 |

33. **R-KRAMPUS-06** Presents are not in play and are not items; only this challenge's rules add or remove their counters (Q-40).
34. **R-KRAMPUS-07** **All I Want for Christmas**: when a player would gain a monster's rewards or a soul, they may forgo it and put that many counters on the present of the same kind instead: ¢ for ¢, loot for loot, treasures for treasures, soul value for a soul (Q-30, Q-32, Q-33).
35. **R-KRAMPUS-08** **What's in the Box?**: a present at 0 counters is destroyed. Then the top 3 cards of the monster deck (Normal) or the top 4 (Hard, Ultra Hard) go into minion slots, one at a time (R-CHAL-09, R-CHAL-12).
36. **R-KRAMPUS-09** Counters never go below 0; removing counters from a destroyed present does nothing. When the fourth present is destroyed, the challenge is lost at once (R-KRAMPUS-01).
37. **R-KRAMPUS-10** **Happy Holidays!**: the first time the players hold 5 souls (Normal), 6 (Hard) or 7 (Ultra Hard) between them, every present still there flips face up and becomes an item; for each one, the active player picks the player who gains it (Q-30, Q-40).
38. **R-KRAMPUS-11** Pilfering Krampus can't be attacked and can't take damage while the players hold fewer souls between them than 5 (Normal), 6 (Hard) or 7 (Ultra Hard), or while a minion is in play (Q-30).
39. **R-KRAMPUS-12** As each turn starts, the active player rolls a D6 and removes counters from a present (a triggered ability of the boss; it uses the stack). On Hard they roll once more for every 2 minions in play; on Ultra Hard once more for each minion (Q-41).

| Roll | Normal | Hard | Ultra Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 from ¢ | 5 from ¢ | 8 from ¢ |
| 2 | 4 from ¢ | 5 from ¢ | 2 from loot |
| 3 | 1 from loot | 2 from loot | 2 from loot |
| 4 | 1 from loot | 2 from loot | 1 from treasure |
| 5 | 1 from treasure | 1 from treasure | 1 from treasure |
| 6 | 1 from soul | 1 from soul | 1 from soul |

40. **R-KRAMPUS-13** Normal only: while a minion is in play, the active player must attack a random minion once each turn if able (Q-31).
41. **R-KRAMPUS-14** Every difficulty: players gain no rewards and no souls when a minion dies; a minion with a soul icon goes to discard.
42. **R-KRAMPUS-15** The regular Krampus (Four Souls+) stays in the monster deck (Q-36).
43. **R-KRAMPUS-16** The competitive variant (2–4 players, personal present slots, its own boss card) is not supported yet (roadmap 14.28).
