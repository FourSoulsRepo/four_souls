# Winning and bartering

Sources: S-OFF Winning the Game, Bartering; S-RU Победа в игре, Обмен.

## Winning

1. **R-WIN-01** A player with a total soul value of 4 or more wins; effects may change the target.
2. **R-WIN-02** The win check does not use the stack; it happens between resolutions.
3. **R-WIN-03** When a player wins, everyone else loses; several winners at once tie.
4. **R-WIN-04** An ability that makes a player win does so after it resolves.

## Bartering

5. **R-BARTER-01** Players may trade ¢ for favors and promises at any time; both sides must agree.
6. **R-BARTER-02** Items and loot cards cannot be traded.
7. **R-BARTER-03** Bartering does not use the stack.
8. **R-BARTER-04** Promises are not binding; the game does not enforce them.
