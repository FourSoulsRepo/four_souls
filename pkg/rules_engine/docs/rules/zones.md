# Zones

Sources: S-OFF Game Zones; S-RU Игровые Зоны.

1. **R-ZONE-01** A card that moves to another zone becomes a new object; abilities targeting the old object fizzle.
2. **R-ZONE-02** **In play** holds everything players control and the top card of every shop, monster and room slot. It is public.
3. **R-ZONE-03** Abilities only see objects in play, unless stated.
4. **R-ZONE-04** Each deck holds only its own card type and is hidden; its order changes only when an ability says so.
5. **R-ZONE-05** An empty deck is refilled at once by shuffling its discard pile.
6. **R-ZONE-06** Each deck has its discard pile; a card goes on top of the matching pile. A card without a matching pile is removed from the game.
7. **R-ZONE-07** Discard piles are public, including their size; their order changes only when an ability says so.
8. **R-ZONE-08** A hand holds only loot cards and is hidden from other players; its size is public.
9. **R-ZONE-09** Putting a non-loot card into a hand does nothing.
10. **R-ZONE-10** A card under another card in a slot is **covered** and not in play. There is no limit to covered cards.
11. **R-ZONE-11** When the covering object leaves play, the card below enters play again (ambushes and trinkets become monsters and items again).
12. **R-ZONE-12** Covered cards are public.
13. **R-ZONE-13** **Outside the game** holds unused characters, starting items and bonus souls, removed cards, and cards put under an object outside a slot.
14. **R-ZONE-14** Cards outside the game have no abilities, except the active bonus souls.
15. **R-ZONE-15** You cannot get a card from outside the game if no copy is there.
16. **R-ZONE-16** Face-up cards outside the game are public; face-down ones are hidden.
