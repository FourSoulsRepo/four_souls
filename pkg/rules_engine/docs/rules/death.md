# Death

Sources: S-OFF Death; S-RU Смерть.

1. **R-DEATH-01** An object at 0 HP (or with a non-number HP) puts its death on the stack the next time a player would get priority, unless its death is already there.
2. **R-DEATH-02** If the death is not prevented or cancelled by the time it resolves, the object dies.
3. **R-DEATH-03** Eternal objects cannot die; their death never goes on the stack.

## Monster death

4. **R-DEATH-04** A dying monster moves out of its slot to a holding zone.
5. **R-DEATH-05** "When a monster dies" triggers that come before rewards trigger.
6. **R-DEATH-06** The active player gains the rewards.
7. **R-DEATH-07** Triggers "after rewards" trigger.
8. **R-DEATH-08** A monster with a soul icon becomes a soul of the active player; otherwise it goes to discard. One that cannot go to discard returns to its slot.
9. **R-DEATH-09** Monster slots are refilled.
10. **R-DEATH-10** These steps do not use the stack; priority passes only when triggers are added.
11. **R-DEATH-11** Other attackable objects that die follow the same steps, but do not count as monsters for triggers.

## Player death

12. **R-DEATH-12** When the active player dies, their attack, purchase or end declaration stops and their attack is cancelled.
13. **R-DEATH-13** "When a player dies" triggers come before the death penalty unless they say otherwise.
14. **R-DEATH-14** Death penalty: destroy a non-eternal item of your choice, discard a loot card, lose 1¢, deactivate everything you control that has a ↷ ability. Skip any part you cannot pay.
15. **R-DEATH-15** A non-active player's death ends after the penalty.
16. **R-DEATH-16** The active player continues: the stack fully resolves and empty slots refill, then the turn goes to the end phase.
17. **R-DEATH-17** A player dies at most once per turn.
18. **R-DEATH-18** Dead players cannot be healed by abilities; everyone, dead or alive, heals to full at the end of every turn (R-TURN-13).
19. **R-DEATH-19** A player who died on another player's turn is alive again when that turn ends.
